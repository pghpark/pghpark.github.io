### [Template Maker]({{ "/tools/template-maker/" | prepend: site.baseurl }})

Turn a photo into an editable text template. It recognises Traditional Chinese (繁體中文) and English,
removes the original text, and lets you edit, save and export to PNG, JPEG, WebP, SVG, searchable PDF
or a layered Photoshop PSD with live text layers.

The app is in English (UK) and 繁體中文. Switch at the top right, or open it straight in
[繁體中文]({{ "/tools/template-maker/?lang=zh" | prepend: site.baseurl }}) or
[English]({{ "/tools/template-maker/?lang=en" | prepend: site.baseurl }}).
Photos can be JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF, BMP or AVIF.
