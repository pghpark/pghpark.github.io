      PDFs keep real, searchable text; in PowerPoint, Canva and Photoshop the text stays editable. On a phone, files go straight to the Save / Share sheet.

      Private, and works offline

      Stays on your phoneWorks offlineOne download

      Everything runs on your phone: photos are never uploaded, and saved templates stay on your device. The first photo downloads the text reader once (about 46 MB, best on Wi‑Fi, about a minute); after that it is stored and works without a connection.
